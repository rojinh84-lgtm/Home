About Tehran

Tehran is the vibrant capital of Iran and one of the largest cities in the Middle East. Located at the foot of the Alborz Mountains, the city is known for its unique combination of modern life, rich history, and diverse culture. From the historic palaces and traditional bazaars to modern streets, museums, cafés, and busy neighborhoods, Tehran offers a fascinating contrast between the past and the present. With its energetic atmosphere, beautiful mountain views, and millions of people from different backgrounds, Tehran is a city full of life, stories, and unforgettable experiences.
